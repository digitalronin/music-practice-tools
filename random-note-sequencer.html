How it works
A 4-beat count-in leads you in. All selected notes are played once before a new random round begins. Each press of Start shuffles a fresh random order; Stop pauses the exercise. Your tempo, lookahead and note selection are remembered between visits. Notes you untick in the keyboard below stop appearing once the current sequence finishes — handy for drilling just the sharps and flats, for example.
